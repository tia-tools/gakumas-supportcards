# A card's 点数 is its maximum-performance expected parameter gain under an all-SP-lesson reference route

The 凸別点数一覧 needs one number per (card, 凸) that players can compare. We define a point as one parameter (Vo+Da+Vi summed) the card is expected to add over one run of the target scenario, computed as effect value × the number of times the route profile says that trigger occurs, with every conditional trigger (stat ≥ N, cards ≥ N, stamina ratio) treated as always satisfied, and with the route assuming every lesson taken is an SP lesson (so SPレッスン発生率+ effects score 0 and 通常レッスン-only effects score 0). Card events and card-granted P-items count once and trigger-limit times respectively. Alternatives rejected: hand-tuned ability weights (opinion, unexplainable), full produce simulation (needs an engine per scenario, slow to keep current), fulfilment ratios for conditional triggers (more knobs, and the community's de facto standard is maximum performance).

Source: user decisions D1, D4, D10, D11, D12 in the grill-me session of 2026-09-16 (`docs/plans/EXECPLAN_SUPPORT_CARD_SCORE_TABLE.md` § Decision Log). Not yet validated against community-published numbers; validation is milestone M2 of that plan.

Addendum 2026-09-21 (status unchanged, scope refined): "every conditional trigger treated as always satisfied" is now the default rather than a fixed rule. The user observed that some conditions are easy on one route and hard on another — こんにゃくなきもだめし needs a deck of 20 or more skill cards at an SP lesson, plausible under 差し入れ育成 and hard under the deletion-heavy 汎用コンテ育成 — so a condition becomes a count bounded by its occasion that starts at the maximum, that the player can lower in the panel, and that a route profile may lower as its shipped default when the user is confident (every profile ships at the maximum for now). This is not the "fulfilment ratios" alternative rejected above: there are no ratios and no new mandatory knobs, the published default is still maximum performance, and an effect's own per-run cap still applies on top. Source: user decisions C2 and C7, grill-me session 2026-09-21 (`docs/plans/EXECPLAN_COUNTING_MODEL.md` § Decision Log).

Addendum 2026-09-28 (status unchanged, scope refined and validation stated): lesson-end triggers are bound to one stat (`lesson_{vocal,dance,visual}`), and each lesson trains one stat the player picks by deck build, so a route profile carries lesson-split presets rather than one split (H.I.F.: 7/1/0, 7/0/1, 1/7/0, 0/7/1, 1/0/7, 0/1/7 over its eight lessons), and each card is scored under the preset that gives it the highest total — the maximum-performance reading for a card of a given stat; the パラメータボーナス base follows the same split. This is why a Vo card and a Vi card in one table are scored under different splits. Rejected: one fixed split per profile (the right split depends on the build, not the route). The validation the Source line above deferred to M2 was not against community-published numbers — the user does not track them — but a line-by-line check by the user of the engine's breakdowns for ten golden cards chosen to cover every mechanism, 38 of 38 lines confirmed; the numbers are therefore validated for inputs and arithmetic, not against third-party tables. Source: decisions D25 (user observation) and D26 (user presets, agent best-preset rule), 2026-09-19, and the golden check of 2026-09-28 (`docs/plans/EXECPLAN_SUPPORT_CARD_SCORE_TABLE.md` § Decision Log, § Artifacts).

Addendum 2026-09-29 (status unchanged, scope refined): the パラメータボーナス base's 選抜試験 term no longer uses the lesson share as a proxy for a stat's share of the exam's distributed rewards. That share is a player input in role terms — main, sub and other stat of the split that scores the card — in tenths summing to 10, default 2 : 7 : 1 (URL `a=<main>.<sub>.<other>`), so under the default H.I.F.'s bases are 1021 / 799 / 420 instead of 1359 / 511 / 370 and every card with パラメータボーナス+ (87) scores lower than before; only that term changed — the lesson and sub-parameter terms stay expected values and 本戦 rounds give no parameter. Role terms rather than absolute Vo/Da/Vi because under best-preset scoring "sub" is a different stat per card. Validation as before: the user re-confirmed the four moved golden lines (大切な思い出 6.5 % and 8.5 % × 1021, おやすみのふたり 4.4 % and 6.4 % × 1021), the eight other golden cards unchanged by test. Source: user decisions A3–A6 (grill session 2026-09-28) and the confirmation of 2026-09-28, `docs/plans/EXECPLAN_SCORE_ADJUSTMENTS.md` § Decision Log and § Progress (Milestone 2).

Addendum 2026-09-29, second (status unchanged, scope refined): the audition share's default belongs to the route profile, and the player's share is kept per profile. H.I.F. gains a fourth profile, 極振りコンテ育成 — all eight lessons on one stat, the 選抜試験's score sent mostly to one of the two others, default share 1 : 9 : 0, every other count that of 汎用コンテ育成 — so under 1 : 9 : 0 its bases are 1050 (the trained stat), 820 (sub) and 370 (other); every other profile keeps 2 : 7 : 1. Under 8/0/0 the two untrained stats tie, and the counts cannot say which one the player sends the score to, so a lesson-split preset may name its `sub`, which wins a tie (never a stat with more lessons); the profile ships six presets, Vo8 with Da or Vi as sub and so on, and each card is scored under its best one as before. Rejected: three presets with the old tie order Vo > Da > Vi, which would let a Vi card be the sub under Vo8 but never under Da8. A share the player sets belongs to the profile it was set under: switching profile shows that profile's own share (its default unless set), and switching back restores the first; in the URL the selected profile's share is `a=` and another profile's `a.<profile id>=`. Source: user request and answers of 2026-09-29 (6 named presets, counts copied from 汎用コンテ育成, the share kept per profile).
