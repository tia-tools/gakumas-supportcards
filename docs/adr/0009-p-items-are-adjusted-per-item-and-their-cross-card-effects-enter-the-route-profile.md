# A P-item is adjusted per item, and what it does for other cards enters the route profile, never the granting card's 点数

A support card can grant a P-item (Pアイテム) that fires on its own trigger up to `fireLimit` times per run. Two things about it are the player's to adjust, and both stay inside the definition of a 点数 as one card's marginal gain under a route profile (ADR 0001). First, an item's fires are capped per item: the page's 「Pアイテム」 panel offers, for every item of a card in view, a cap that the engine applies as one more minimum on that item's effects (`itemCaps`, URL `i.<itemId>`) — never a raise — counted under the lesson split in force (the selected preset, or under 「カードごとに最適」 the preset that favours the item). Second, an item that grants P-drinks is counted for the deck, not for its card: a ticked item (`d.<itemId>=1`) adds fires × drinks per fire (the quantity is read from the reward id, `…-02_02` = 2; an unreadable quantity stops the generator) to the run's Pドリンク獲得 count, which every card's Pドリンク獲得時 skill is then scored against, and the granting card's own number does not change. Items that grant skill cards are left out: スキルカード獲得 is narrowed by filters the generator cannot evaluate for a granted card, so any number would be a guess (ADR 0005). Rejected: crediting the granting card with what it unlocks in other cards (needs a deck assumption no number in the table makes); a free per-item count that could exceed the route's counts (goes stale when a shared count is lowered, and can claim fires the route's filters deny); a flat "items in the deck" list without per-item numbers.

Source: user decisions A8–A11 and A25, agent decisions A16, A17 and A22 (the last from a Codex review), 2026-09-28 and 2026-09-29, `docs/plans/EXECPLAN_SCORE_ADJUSTMENTS.md` § Decision Log. Evidence: a real-data test pins that ふわふわでもこもこ ticked adds 14 drinks under 差し入れ育成 and +70 to おい、来てやったぞ！ at 凸4 with +0 to its own card, 2 drinks under Vo7/Da1/Vi0 and 0 under Vo7/Da0/Vi1, and that capping びっくり仮装グッズ at 1 costs はっぴぃはろうぃ～～ん！ 20 (`data/scenarios/scenarios.test.ts`); the stability check reported 0 unexplained moves when the items were added to 49 cards.
